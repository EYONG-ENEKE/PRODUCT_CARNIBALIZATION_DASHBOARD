# PRODUCT_CARNIBALIZATION_DASHBOARD
Product Cannibalization Analytics Dashboard is a Python and Streamlit-based data analytics project designed to identify products that may be at risk of promotional cannibalization. It analyzes promotional frequency, discount depth, product categories, channels, and promotional trends to identify products receiving unusually frequent or deep promotions.  The dashboard transforms raw promotion data into interactive KPIs, visual analytics, risk classifications, and a product cannibalization matrix, helping business teams evaluate promotional strategies and identify areas requiring further investigation.  Note: The project provides a risk-screening approach, not definitive proof of cannibalization. Confirming actual cannibalization would require transaction-level sales, units sold, customer/product substitution, and margin data.
